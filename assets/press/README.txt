USING THE LOGO

- Use the full logo on dark backgrounds only. Its scuff marks are drawn in the Night color and show up as dark specks on light backgrounds.
- On light or busy backgrounds, use the compact wordmark.
- Leave clear space around the logo at least as tall as the padlock.
- On screen, don't use the full logo narrower than 240 px. Below that, use the compact wordmark.
- Don't recolor, stretch, rotate, outline or add shadows or 3D effects. The HEIST tape is always Loot Gold (#FFB000).
- Don't translate HUSH or HEIST, and don't rebuild the name in another font as a logo.
- In text, the name is always Hush Heist: two words, both capitalized. Never "HushHeist" or "Hush" on its own.


KEY ART AND SCREENSHOTS

The key art is staged and rendered in the game's own engine, with a little artistic license (the mic and the drone are bigger than in the game). The screenshots show the game itself.
You may crop and resize everything for your coverage. No credit needed, but "Hush Heist" with a link to the Steam page is appreciated.


VIDEO AND STREAMING POLICY

- Monetization welcome. Stream, record and monetize Hush Heist on YouTube, Twitch, TikTok, Kick and anywhere else. No need to ask.
- No Content ID claims. All music and sound effects are generated by the game's own code. There's no licensed music, and we will never register the soundtrack with Content ID.
- Credit is optional. A link to the Steam page helps a lot once it's live.
- Keys. Press and creators will be able to request keys by email once the Steam page is live. It's a multiplayer game, so we send keys for the whole crew when we can. No strings: no review or score required.
- Your crew's voices are theirs. Please make sure everyone you play with is okay with being recorded.
- Say what you got from us. A free key counts: mention "key provided by the developer" in the video or on stream, not just in the description. If we paid you, also use your platform's tools (YouTube paid promotion, Twitch Branded Content, TikTok commercial content disclosure) and say it at the start of a stream and again every 30–60 minutes.
- Please don't share keys or builds before launch, publish before the embargo in your email, or pass off AI-generated images or voices as footage from the game.
