ABOUT HUSH HEIST (boilerplate, ~90 words)

Hush Heist is a heist party game for 2–7 players on Windows and Mac, made by Matyáš Kouřil, a solo developer in the Czech Republic. Thieves rob a building at night, carrying loot on stretchy rubber arms and talking on proximity voice chat. One friend plays the guard: watching cameras, listening through ceiling microphones, locking doors and flying a taser drone. Whatever the thieves say near a mic, the guard hears. No one wants to guard? Watchman System 3000, the computer, takes the shift. Every round ends on a tabloid front page. More at startheist.com.


ABOUT THE GAME

Hush Heist is a party game about a nighttime robbery where your own mouth is the weak spot. The crew breaks into a department store, a villa, a museum, a bank, a casino or a TV studio, grabs whatever isn't bolted down and hauls it to the van on rubber arms. The guard sits in an office they can't leave, with cameras, ceiling mics, door locks and a taser drone.

A whisper reaches a mic from about 3 meters (10 feet) away, normal talk from about 9 meters (30 feet) and a shout from about 13 meters (43 feet). Each mic has a REC light that pulses while it hears you. A mic doesn't call the police; it just tells the guard where you are. Get spotted on camera after the first 40 seconds, get tased or end up in the cell, and SWAT is seven minutes out. Whoever misses the van is arrested. Then everyone heads back to the pub to argue about whose fault it was.


KEY FEATURES

1. Your voice gives you away.
   Friends hear you by distance and walls muffle you. A ceiling mic picks up a whisper only from right below it, a shout from across the room, and the guard hears whatever you say near a mic. No mic? Type in chat and your character mumbles it out loud.

2. One friend is the guard.
   Cameras, ceiling mics, door locks, an alarm, lights-out, a PA system and a drone with a taser, all run from an office the guard can't leave. Nobody wants to guard? Watchman System 3000, the computer, takes the shift.

3. Rubber arms, real falls.
   Loot hangs off arms that stretch, squeak and snap. A safe or a statue takes two. Banana peels, thrown chairs and sprint collisions knock you flat with real physics.

4. SWAT is seven minutes out.
   Get spotted on camera, tased or locked in the cell and the countdown starts. Whoever misses the van is arrested and loses their cut.

5. The pub between jobs.
   Throw a knife at tonight's job, climb into the trash bin to play guard, spend the crew kitty at the boss's table and your own cut on hats at the coat rack. Miss the boss's quota and you're thrown across the pub.

6. Every night is different.
   A department store plus villas, museums, banks, casinos and TV studios rebuilt every round, night events like a thunderstorm, and a tabloid front page starring your crew's most embarrassing moment, ready to save as a GIF.


DID YOU USE AI?

Hush Heist is made by one developer. Every model, material, song and sound effect in the game is generated by the game's own code: no generative AI art, music, sound or voices, and no stock assets. I used AI coding assistants while programming. The German, Polish, Spanish and Simplified Chinese translations were made with an AI tool, and the Steam page will say so too.
