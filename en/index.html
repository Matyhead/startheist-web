The pitch

A heist where your mouth is the weak spot

Hush Heist is about robbing a store quietly while the adrenaline is pumping and your buddy is yelling that he found a statue. Thieves hear each other by distance: whisper in a corner and only the guy next to you hears it. But the rooms have microphones, and the guard hears everything said near them.

Proximity voice

Whisper in your partner's ear or shout across the store. Walls and closed doors muffle your voice, and a siren drowns it out completely.

The guard is listening

Mics pick up footsteps, crowbars, a smashed vase and every word. Cameras and motion sensors watch, a taser drone hunts. The guard is a friend, or the computer.

Steal everything

TVs, gold bars, a sarcophagus, a golden toilet, a JACKPOT machine, cardboard Mr. Novák. Armchairs, plants and barrels too. Heavy stuff takes two, and rubber arms stretch until they snap.

Two players, three, or just you

Nobody wants to guard? The computer will.

If nobody climbs into the bin at the pub, Watchman System 3000 takes the guard's chair. It gets the same cameras, locks and drone a human does, so you can play as a duo, as an all-thief crew, or solo.

  • It hears what you say near a mic. Give yourself away and you get A MIC HEARD YOU, plus a chime from the ceiling speaker.
  • It locks doors, sets off the alarm or kills the lights, then sends an autopilot drone through the hatches above the doors, straight to the alert. It beeps while it aims, and sometimes it misses.
  • Fool it like a human. Hide in a locker, freeze among the mannequins, or knock the drone out of the air with a thrown object and steal it.
  • It scales with your crew. Gentler on a lone thief, toughest on three or more.

The guard hits the lights

Darkness hides you. Your headlamp gives you away.

The guard clicks a light bulb on the map and the room goes dark for 25 seconds. Cameras can barely see you in the dark, but you can't see the loot either. Switch on your headlamp and the cameras and drone see you just fine.

  • Headlamp on L. Your crew sees the beam from across the room. So does the camera.
  • Without a headlamp, a camera only spots you in the dark from four meters away.
  • In a thunderstorm, lightning kills the lights on its own, and thunder drowns out footsteps and voices near the mics.

How a round goes

Pub at night, heist after dark, headlines by morning

  1. 0

    Evening · the pub

    Pick the job

    At The Golden Crook you throw a knife at the job you want on the board. The bin decides who guards: whoever sits in it is the guard. Feel free to shove someone in. Empty bin? The computer guards.

  2. 1

    Afternoon · 90 seconds

    Prep

    The guard places cameras, mics and motion sensors on the map. Meanwhile the thieves vote at the van on how to get in and pick their tools. The guard can't hear them, so this is the time to plan.

  3. 2

    Night · 7 minutes

    The heist

    The “plumbers'” van parks by the entrance. You have seven minutes to get loot worth the target into it. The guard locks doors, kills lights, sets off alarms and hunts you with the drone. The music ramps up when things get hairy. Every night is different: different loot, different locked doors, the odd thunderstorm.

  4. 3

    Morning

    The paper

    The round ends with the front page of The Grapevine: how much you stole, what you broke, who slipped. And instead of a photo, it plays a clip of your most embarrassing moment.

Before the job

The lobby is a pub called The Golden Crook

No menu full of buttons. Before the job, the crew runs around a 90s dive bar, throws bottles and beer mugs at each other, shoves each other around and argues about what to rob tonight.

  • Whoever sits in the bin is the guard. Climb in yourself or get shoved in by your crew. Whoever guarded last time gets spat out. An empty bin means the computer guards.
  • Pick the job by throwing a knife at a photo on the boss's board. The handle is your color and it sticks with a satisfying wobble. Once everyone has thrown, the boss points at the winner and you're off.
  • At the mirror rack you choose what goes on your head and over your eyes. More pieces unlock with the money you steal (or protect, as the guard), and the crew sees them in the pub, during the heist, in photos and in the paper.
  • The Achievements board shows who already has Workplace Accident (slip with something big in your hands) or Art Collector (drive off with just a rubber duck). There are 21 to collect.

The boss wants his cut

A quota for the night, a shop for your loot

The boss sets your crew a quota over three jobs. Everything you haul away in the van counts toward it and lands in the crew's shared kitty, which you spend right at his table.

  • The shop has extra spray and jammer charges, a better crowbar, a sharper cutter, an extra pocket, longer arms, plus a banana peel and a whoopee cushion waiting by the van.
  • Anyone can buy, with everyone's money. Whoever blows the kitty on whoopee cushions has some explaining to do.
  • For every two upgrades you buy, the insurance company gets the guard an extra camera, sensor or mic. It keeps things fair.
  • Hit the quota and the boss celebrates, then raises the bar. Miss it and you're fired. The boss throws the whole crew across the pub and everything resets.

Where to tonight

A different place every night

Koruna Department Store is tuned down to the last shelf. Villas, museums, banks, casinos and TV stations are built differently every time: different rooms, different loot, different locked doors. Some have two floors, and then you're hauling the safe down the stairs together.

Department store 1 floor

Electronics, jewelry, a gallery with a statue and a rubber duck displayed as art.

Villa 1–2 floors

A golden toilet in the bathroom, wine in the cellar, a portrait of the owner worth pocket change. A safe in the bedroom upstairs.

Museum 1–2 floors

A dinosaur skeleton, a sarcophagus with a mummy and a banana taped to the wall, worth $12,000.

Bank 1–2 floors

A vault behind steel doors: crowbar or vents. Inside: gold bars, money bags and a piggy bank labeled “emergency fund”.

Casino 1–2 floors

Roulette, rows of slots and, in the VIP lounge, a golden JACKPOT machine that takes two to carry. Grab a slot machine and it rings like a win, right into the guard's mic.

TV station 1–2 floors

A newsroom with a studio camera that takes two, a game show with a prize wheel, a makeup room and, in the archive, the only tape of New Year's Eve 1996.

Two sides

Which side are you on?

It's best with three thieves against one guard. The bin at the pub decides who guards, and last round's guard steals next time. If nobody guards, the computer does.

Thieves

Get the loot into the van

  • Everyone gets one tool: spray, jammer, crowbar or glass cutter. You'll need each other.
  • Hide in a locker, crawl through the vents, or freeze among the mannequins and the cameras won't tell you apart.
  • Caught teammates get hauled off to the cell. You can run back and pick the lock.
  • In the dark, switch on your headlamp. You'll see the loot, but the cameras will see you.
  • Sprinting leaves you out of breath, and the mics can hear you panting. Sometimes slow is smart.

You win when the target value is in the van. For three thieves it's $25,000; smaller crews get a lower target.

Guard

Hold the fort from the office

  • At the computer you watch the cameras and the building map and listen to the mics.
  • Lock doors, kill the lights and trigger the alarm: it deafens the thieves in the room and flips their controls.
  • Put on the FPV goggles and hunt thieves with a taser drone. Watch the battery and don't fly outside, you'll lose signal. And whatever you do, don't let them steal it.
  • Pick a room and talk into it over the PA. Caught thieves yell at you from the cell and bang on the glass.
  • Protected loot unlocks pieces on the mirror rack, three of them guard-only: a uniform cap, mirrored shades and a guard's mustache.

You win when every thief is in the cell, or time runs out.

Clips from the game

And why it's so stupidly fun

Physics, rubber arms and voice chat. You can't plan the best moments.

Helium

Balloons in the pub, the villa and the museum. One breath and you sound like a cartoon for twenty seconds. Everyone hears it, the guard too, through the mic.

PA and the cell

The guard talks into a chosen room through the ceiling speaker. Caught thieves yell back from the cell and bang on the glass.

Throw with care

Throw something hard, hard enough, and it knocks over a buddy, smashes a camera or brings down the guard's drone. A pillow won't.

Thief gear

Four tools, one each

Everyone picks one tool before the round, and the boss sells upgrades. Hover over a clip to see it in action.

Spray 3 uses

Paint a camera or sensor black. The guard is left staring at nothing.

Jammer 2 uses

Nearby mics hear only static for a while. Cameras still see you.

Crowbar unlimited

Pops locked doors, windows and display cases fast. It's loud, though.

Glass cutter unlimited

Quietly cuts through window or display glass. Takes longer.

    The host clicks HOST GAME and invites friends from the pub with Esc. Everyone else clicks the Steam invite, or FIND GAME. First time? HOW TO PLAY has a short tutorial for both thief and guard.

FAQ

When something's off

How many players do we need?

Just one. Solo, you play a thief against the computer (TRAINING). With two, you can both steal while the computer guards, or one of you guards. It's best with three thieves and one guard, and you can go up to seven.

How do I run it on a Mac?

Download the Mac version (HushHeist-mac.zip), it runs on Apple Silicon and Intel. The game isn't signed by Apple yet, so macOS blocks it the first time: click Done, open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to HushHeist. On macOS 14 and older you can also right-click HushHeist.app and choose Open. If macOS says the app is damaged, type xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine and a space in Terminal, drag HushHeist.app into the window and press Enter. The game window appears once you answer the microphone prompt.

My friends can't hear me

On Windows, open Settings › Privacy & security › Microphone and allow desktop apps. On a Mac, open System Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone and turn on both Hush Heist and Steam. In the game, SETTINGS › MICROPHONE lets you pick your mic and hit TEST: it records a few seconds and plays them right back. Someone too loud? Esc › PLAYER VOICES sets each player's volume.

I can't see my friend's game in the list

New games take a few seconds to show up on Steam, so click REFRESH. Invites are more reliable: the host presses Esc in the pub and clicks INVITE FRIENDS.

What if the host's game crashes?

The next player takes over and the crew meets back at the pub. Whatever you already loaded counts toward the kitty and the boss's quota. Whoever dropped clicks REJOIN and waits in the pub until the round ends.

The game stutters

Esc › SETTINGS › DISPLAY & MOUSE and set graphics to LOW. F11 toggles fullscreen (Option+Enter on a Mac).

What languages does it support?

English by default. Czech is one click away: SETTINGS, then Language / Jazyk at the very top. The game remembers your choice.

Where are the photo booth pictures and newspaper GIFs?

In Pictures › Hush Heist (Windows and Mac). The photo booth picture is saved for everyone in the pub, the GIF for whoever clicks SAVE VIDEO.

Can I play without Steam?

Over a local network, yes: LOCAL NETWORK in the menu. Over the internet it's Steam only for now.
