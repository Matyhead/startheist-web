The pitch

A heist where your own mouth gives you away

Thieves steal

At night they break into a department store, villa, museum, bank, casino or TV station. They have seven minutes to get the loot into the van. Two of you, three, or the whole crew.

The guard watches

Sits in the office at the cameras, listens to the mics, locks doors, kills the lights and hunts thieves with a taser drone. A friend guards, and if nobody wants to, the computer does.

Your mouth snitches

Thieves hear each other by distance, as if they stood side by side. But whatever you say under a mic, the guard hears too. One loud “the safe's over here!” is all it takes.

Voice and bugs

Whisper. The guard is listening.

Whisper in your partner's ear or yell across the whole floor. Your voice carries by distance, like in a real building. But there are mics in the ceiling, and whatever is said near them, the guard hears word for word.

  • Walls and closed doors muffle your voice, an alarm drowns it out completely.
  • Mics pick up footsteps too, the crowbar, a smashed vase and heavy things being dragged. The guard sees which one is blinking on the map.
  • The radio on B carries your voice to the whole crew, wherever they are.
  • The jammer floods nearby mics with static for a while. The cameras still see you.
  • Sprinting leaves you out of breath and the mic hears you panting.
  • Helium from a balloon: twenty seconds of cartoon voice. Everyone hears it, the guard included.
  • Got caught? Yell at the guard through the cell glass. The guard answers over the PA in any room.

Rubber arms and two-person carries

Rubber arms. A safe for two.

You hold loot in palms glued to its sides. Walk away from it and your arms stretch, thin out and squeak like rubber. Go too far and they snap: boing, a noise for the mics, and a few seconds of arms flopping on the floor.

  • Statue, safe, golden JACKPOT machine: you can't budge them alone. Two of you can, until everyone pulls a different way.
  • A TV goes faster with two and every extra carrier helps.
  • Small things go in your four pockets, big ones in your hands on a spring. They bump into walls, and the heavier they are, the slower you walk.
  • You can grab and throw almost anything: armchairs, plants, mannequins, clothes racks. Doors and drawers are pulled by hand.

Physics falls

Every fall is real physics

Slip on a banana peel, step into foam from a smashed extinguisher, get hit by a thrown chair or the drone's taser. Your body flies wherever the hit sends it and tumbles all the way down the stairs. Then you get up and pretend nothing happened.

  • You go down from a banana, a wet floor, a thrown chair, bottle or ball, a proper shove, the taser and big drops (off the top of a ladder or jumping into the stairwell from upstairs).
  • A cushion or a rubber duck won't knock you over. At night, though, thrown loot will drop your partner, so watch where you aim.
  • The whole crew sees the same fall, online too. And if it's embarrassing enough, it makes the papers.

The guard

Cameras, mics and a taser drone

One player guards from an office they can't leave. During the day they get ninety seconds to place cameras, mics and motion sensors on the map. At night they watch the screens, listen and strike.

The guard wins when every thief is in the cell or time runs out.

Cameras and sensors

A camera that sees a thief for more than a moment raises the alarm. A sensor reports anyone who isn't sneaking.

Microphones

They hear voices, footsteps and crashes. The one where something is happening blinks on the map.

Locks and alarm

Click a door to lock it. An alarm deafens the thieves in the room and flips their controls inside out.

Lights out

A room goes dark for 25 seconds and cameras can barely see. Whoever turns on a headlamp lights up like a beacon.

Taser drone

FPV goggles, momentum and a battery. It flies through hatches above doors and loses signal outside. And if it crashes, the thieves will steal it.

PA and the cell

The guard talks into any room through the ceiling speaker. Caught thieves sit in the cell, and their partners can break them out.

The guard hits the lights

Darkness hides you. Your headlamp doesn't.

  • In the dark a camera only spots you up close. But you can't see the loot.
  • Headlamp on L lights the way. Your partners, the cameras and the drone all see its beam.
  • In a storm lightning kills the lights by itself and thunder drowns out footsteps and voices at the mics.

WATCHMAN SYSTEM 3000

Only two or three of you? The computer guards.

If nobody climbs into the bin at the pub, the computer takes over as the guard. It has the same cameras, mics, locks and drone as a human, so the whole crew can steal, even just the two of you.

  • It hears what you say near a mic, locks doors, sounds the alarm and kills the lights.
  • The autopilot drone follows noise, turns toward anyone it spots and searches the neighbouring rooms.
  • It scales with your crew: softer on a lone thief, toughest on three or more.

Against all that, every thief gets one tool

The crew has to cover for each other. Hover over a clip to see the tool in action.

Spray paint 3 uses

Blacks out a camera or sensor. The guard stares at darkness.

Jammer 2 uses

Nearby mics hear nothing but static for a while.

Crowbar unlimited

Doors, windows and display cases. Fast, but very loud.

Glass cutter unlimited

Cut a hole in the glass by hand. Quiet, but slower.

The lobby is a pub

The Golden Crook

No menu full of buttons. Before the job, the crew runs around a 90s dive bar, throws bottles and chairs at each other and argues about who to rob tonight.

  • Whoever climbs into the bin is the guard. Feel free to shove someone in. An empty bin means the computer guards.
  • You pick the job by throwing a knife into its photo on the boss's board. Most knives wins.
  • The boss sets a quota for three jobs. Make it: he celebrates and the next one is higher. Miss it: he throws you across the whole pub and you start from zero.
  • The crew's shared cash buys upgrades at the boss's table: a better crowbar, longer arms, an extra pocket, a banana peel or a whoopee cushion. For every two upgrades, the insurance company buys the guard an extra piece of kit.
  • The mirror coat rack: hats and glasses unlocked with stolen money (for the guard, with guarded money). The crew sees them at the pub, during the heist and in the paper.

Also in the pub: pool, darts, a one-armed bandit, a jukebox, helium balloons, a photo booth with a polaroid and whisky, the third shot puts you on the floor.

Where to tonight

Six places. Every night different.

Koruna Department Store is tuned down to the last shelf. Villas, museums, banks, casinos and TV stations are built differently every time: different rooms, different loot, different locked doors. Some have two floors, and then you carry the safe down the stairs together.

Department store 1 floor

Electronics, jewellery, a gallery with a statue and a rubber duck on display as art.

Villa 1–2 floors

A golden toilet in the bathroom, wine in the cellar and a bedroom safe upstairs.

Museum 1–2 floors

A dinosaur skeleton, a sarcophagus with a mummy and a banana duct-taped to the wall for $12,000.

Bank 1–2 floors

A steel vault door: crowbar or the vents. Inside, gold bars and a piggy bank labelled “emergency fund”.

Casino 1–2 floors

Roulette, rows of slots and a golden JACKPOT for two. Grab a slot machine and it rings like a win, and the mic hears it.

TV station 1–2 floors

A news studio with a two-person studio camera, a wheel of fortune, make-up room and one New Year's Eve 1996 tape in the archive.

The Grapevine, morning edition

In the morning you make the papers

Every round ends with the front page of The Grapevine tabloid: how much you stole, what you broke, who slipped, who stole the drone and whether the boss fired you. Instead of a photo, it plays a clip of the night's most embarrassing moment.

The SAVE VIDEO button saves that moment as a GIF to your Pictures folder, ready to share. Then the host hits REMATCH and you go again, usually with a different guard.

  • Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), or a Mac with macOS 13 or newer (Apple Silicon; Intel from macOS 11)
  • Steam running and logged in (you don't buy anything there, it just has to be running)
  • A microphone and headphones. Without a mic it's half the fun.

  1. Extract the whole zip

    Windows: right click the zip, Extract All. Don't run the game from inside the zip, the Steam libraries would be missing. Mac: double click the zip to get HushHeist.app.

  2. Start Steam and log in

    In Steam the game shows up as “Spacewar”. That's the test ID for games in development, it's fine.

  3. Launch the game

    Windows: HushHeist.exe. On the blue “Windows protected your PC” screen click More info and Run anyway.

    Mac: macOS blocks it the first time. Click Done, open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. When asked about the microphone, click Allow.

  4. First time?

    HOW TO PLAY has a short intro for both the thief and the guard. The game is in English, Czech is in SETTINGS › Language / Jazyk.

  5. Host and invite your friends

    The host clicks HOST GAME and sends invites from the pub with Esc › INVITE FRIENDS. Everyone else already has the game running and clicks the invite in Steam chat, or FIND GAME in the menu.

    The Steam overlay (Shift+Tab) doesn't work in the demo, because the game isn't launched from Steam. Invite through Esc in the pub.

FAQ

When something's off

How many of us do we need?

Just one. Alone you play a thief against the computer (TRAINING). With two, you can both steal while the computer guards, or one of you guards. It plays best with three thieves and one guard, up to seven players.

My friend clicked the invite and Steam wants to install Spacewar

Hush Heist has to be running before you click the invite. Close the Steam window, start the game and click the invite again. Or pick the host's lobby under FIND GAME (a new one shows up after a few seconds, or hit REFRESH).

macOS says the app is damaged

Open Terminal, type xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine, a space, drag HushHeist.app into the window and press Enter. Then open the game again. On macOS 14 and older you can also right click HushHeist.app and choose Open. The game window appears once you answer the microphone prompt.

My friends can't hear me

On Windows open Settings, Privacy, Microphone and allow desktop apps. On a Mac open System Settings, Privacy & Security, Microphone and turn on Hush Heist and Steam. In the game, pick the right mic in SETTINGS › MICROPHONE and click TEST. Someone too loud? Esc and PLAYER VOICES sets each person's volume.

What if the host's game crashes?

Another player takes over and the crew meets up at the pub. Whatever you managed to load counts toward the cash and the boss's quota. Whoever dropped out clicks REJOIN.

The game is laggy

Esc, SETTINGS, DISPLAY & MOUSE and set graphics to LOW. Fullscreen is F11, on a Mac Option+Enter.

Where are the photo booth pictures and newspaper GIFs?

In Pictures › Hush Heist (on both Windows and Mac). The photo booth picture is saved for everyone in the pub at the time, the newspaper GIF for whoever clicks SAVE VIDEO.

Can I play without Steam?

Over a local network, yes: the menu has LOCAL NETWORK. Over the internet, only through Steam for now.
