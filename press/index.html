Description

About Hush Heist

Boilerplate · about 90 words, free to copy

Hush Heist is a heist party game for 2–7 players on Windows and Mac, made by Matyáš Kouřil, a solo developer in the Czech Republic. Thieves rob a building at night, carrying loot on stretchy rubber arms and talking on proximity voice chat. One friend plays the guard: watching cameras, listening through ceiling microphones, locking doors and flying a taser drone. Whatever the thieves say near a mic, the guard hears. No one wants to guard? Watchman System 3000, the computer, takes the shift. Every round ends on a tabloid front page. More at startheist.com.

About the game

Hush Heist is a party game about a nighttime robbery where your own mouth is the weak spot. The crew breaks into a department store, a villa, a museum, a bank, a casino or a TV studio, grabs whatever isn't bolted down and hauls it to the van on rubber arms. The guard sits in an office they can't leave, with cameras, ceiling mics, door locks and a taser drone.

A whisper reaches a mic from about 3 meters (10 feet) away, normal talk from about 9 meters (30 feet) and a shout from about 13 meters (43 feet). Each mic has a REC light that pulses while it hears you. A mic doesn't call the police; it just tells the guard where you are. Get spotted on camera after the first 40 seconds, get tased or end up in the cell, and SWAT is seven minutes out. Whoever misses the van is arrested. Then everyone heads back to the pub to argue about whose fault it was.

What to write about

Key features

  1. Your voice gives you away. Friends hear you by distance and walls muffle you. A ceiling mic picks up a whisper only from right below it, a shout from across the room, and the guard hears whatever you say near a mic. No mic? Type in chat and your character mumbles it out loud.
  2. One friend is the guard. Cameras, ceiling mics, door locks, an alarm, lights-out, a PA system and a drone with a taser, all run from an office the guard can't leave. Nobody wants to guard? Watchman System 3000, the computer, takes the shift.
  3. Rubber arms, real falls. Loot hangs off arms that stretch, squeak and snap. A safe or a statue takes two. Banana peels, thrown chairs and sprint collisions knock you flat with real physics.
  4. SWAT is seven minutes out. Get spotted on camera, tased or locked in the cell and the countdown starts. Whoever misses the van is arrested and loses their cut.
  5. The pub between jobs. Throw a knife at tonight's job, climb into the trash bin to play guard, spend the crew kitty at the boss's table and your own cut on hats at the coat rack. Miss the boss's quota and you're thrown across the pub.
  6. Every night is different. A department store plus villas, museums, banks, casinos and TV studios rebuilt every round, night events like a thunderstorm, and a tabloid front page starring your crew's most embarrassing moment, ready to save as a GIF.

Logo use

  • Leave clear space around the logo at least as tall as the padlock.
  • On screen, don't use the full logo narrower than 240 px. Below that, use the compact wordmark.
  • Don't recolor, stretch, rotate, outline or add shadows or 3D effects. The HEIST tape is always Loot Gold.
  • Don't translate HUSH or HEIST, and don't rebuild the name in another font as a logo.
  • In text, the name is always Hush Heist: two words, both capitalized.

Streaming

Video & streaming policy

  • Monetization welcome. Stream, record and monetize Hush Heist on YouTube, Twitch, TikTok, Kick and anywhere else. No need to ask.
  • No Content ID claims. All music and sound effects are generated by the game's own code. There's no licensed music, and we will never register the soundtrack with Content ID.
  • Credit is optional. A link to the Steam page helps a lot: .
  • Keys. Press and creators can request keys at . It's a multiplayer game, so we send keys for the whole crew when we can. No strings: no review or score required.
  • Your crew's voices are theirs. Please make sure everyone you play with is okay with being recorded.
  • Say what you got from us. A free key counts: mention “key provided by the developer” in the video or on stream, not just in the description. If we paid you, also use your platform's tools (YouTube paid promotion, Twitch Branded Content, TikTok commercial content disclosure) and say it at the start of a stream and again every 30–60 minutes.
  • Please don't share keys or builds before launch, publish before the embargo in your email, or pass off AI-generated images or voices as footage from the game.

Playing on stream

For creators

  • Host a lobby. Press HOST GAME, then invite your friends from the pub menu (Esc) or through Steam.
  • Talk in game, not on Discord. Proximity voice and the guard's mics are the game. Over Discord, nobody can give themselves away.
  • Rotate the guard. Whoever climbs into the trash bin in the pub plays the guard, and the bin won't take last round's guard. After a round, the host can also press SWAP GUARD. The guard's cameras are where a lot of the jokes land.
  • Press F8 when something great happens. It drops your own marker on Steam's timeline.
  • Turn on Steam's game recording. Hush Heist marks SWAT calls, round ends and front-page moments on the timeline, so clipping after the round is quick.
  • Clean HUD. Settings › Display & Mouse › Clean HUD (for streamers) hides the van and quota readout, key hints and the task line. PLAYER VOICES in the Esc menu sets each friend's volume.
  • OBS works as is. You don't need the in-game “Recording for videos” switches. With MY SCREEN (OBS) on, you won't hear friends who haven't agreed to be recorded.

Moments that tend to work: a whisper right under a mic, rubber arms snapping, the sprint to the van, a drone taser, the front page at the end.

FAQ

Did you use AI?

Hush Heist is made by one developer. Every model, material, song and sound effect in the game is generated by the game's own code: no generative AI art, music, sound or voices, and no stock assets. I used AI coding assistants while programming. The German, Polish, Spanish and Simplified Chinese translations were made with an AI tool, and the Steam page will say so too.

Who made it

About the developer

Hush Heist is made by Matyáš Kouřil, a solo developer from České Budějovice, Czech Republic. The models, materials, music and sound effects, down to the jazz theme, are all generated by the game's own code in Godot 4.
